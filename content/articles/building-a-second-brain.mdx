# Building a Second Brain

The concept of a "Second Brain" comes from Tiago Forte's work on personal knowledge management.

## The PARA Method

Organize information into four categories:

1. **Projects** - Current work with clear goals
2. **Areas** - Ongoing responsibilities  
3. **Resources** - Topics of interest
4. **Archives** - Completed or inactive items

## Key Principles

- **Organize for action**, not by topic
- **Capture** ideas when they occur
- **Distill** notes to their essence
- **Express** your knowledge
